# Viability memo: what this concept would do in the Category Expansion seat

To: Fleek hiring team (Special Projects Lead - Category Expansion). From: Ayomide Ahmed. Independent concept; not an official Fleek product; all numbers synthetic.

## Read of the seat
From the public role description: the seat opens new sourcing geographies and categories, and opening a geography requires importer of record, freight, duties and last mile to be solved with viable economics. I read that as a gatekeeping job as much as a deal-making one: say yes quickly when a route is real, and refuse cleanly when it isn't yet.

GBP 5M of illustrative annual GMV on pilot Route A would need 666,667 kept units, 57,871 orders and 552 lots (552,000 kg) a year, with about GBP 0.75M of cash tied up. That is what it would take, not evidence buyers would take it.

## How I'd use it in the first 90 days (plan, TO TEST)
1. Weeks 1-3: agree the gate list and margin/capital floors with finance; load real (internal) quotes for current candidate routes.
2. Weeks 4-8: run one pilot lot on the best route that clears all gates; compare actual yield, returns and freight to the assumptions.
3. Weeks 9-12: decide scale-gate, hold or kill per route from the logged decisions; feed actuals back as new dated sources.
