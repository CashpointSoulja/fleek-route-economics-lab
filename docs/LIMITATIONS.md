# Limitations

- **Not advice.** Duty and VAT logic is a simplified illustration. It is not a customs classification, tariff ruling, origin determination, valuation method or tax/legal advice.
- **Synthetic.** All routes, currencies, suppliers and numbers are invented. No Fleek data.
- **No demand model.** The scale view says what a GMV target would require, never whether buyers want it.
- **Single lot cycle.** Cash at scale = lots in flight × peak cash per lot; it ignores overlap savings, credit terms, inventory carried across cycles and working-capital financing.
- **Flat rates.** No tiered freight, minimum charges, container fill, duty relief, preferential origin, anti-dumping, excise, currency hedging or FX on payout timing.
- **Even selling.** Sales are spread evenly over the window; real sell-through is front- or back-loaded.
- **Evidence is self-declared.** The app checks that a source and date exist and are fresh, not that the source is true.
- **Browser storage only.** State lives in localStorage on one device; the decision log is not tamper-proof or shared.
- **Two routes.** Comparison is fixed at two.
